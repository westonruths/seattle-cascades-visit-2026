Sunflower maze alternative

Lora Lee’s A‑Maze‑Zing Sunflowers

A distinct Duvall farm option with a large sunflower maze, open Sunday into the evening. It is the best alternative if the group wants sunflowers but would rather preserve a real midday nap and go after lunch or toward golden hour.

Duvall · ~30 min plus rural roads Sunday 10 a.m.–8 p.m. ~$106 group · ages 4 and under free Official sunflower maze page ↗

Activity-heavy farm alternative

Bob’s Corn Sunflower Experience

More built-in play than a typical flower-field visit—hayride, slides, playground, sand pit, and corn crib—at the lowest ticket price. Choose it when the kids want farm activities without committing to the scale and noise of the fair.

Snohomish · ~25 min 10 a.m.–6 p.m. · last entry 4:30 ~$76.65 group · before fees/tax Official experience page ↗

Waterfront alternative

Steamship Sunday · Virginia V

A short, memorable Lake Union cruise on a historic steamship—an especially good fit if we want a lower-commitment Sunday with boats, city views, and a real Seattle experience. Choose one cruise, then pair it with an easy lunch or nearby waterfront walk rather than stacking it with the fair.

Lake Union · free cruise Board 10:30 / 12:30 · depart 11 / 1 1-hour cruise · snacks and drinks available Official Virginia V details ↗

Full-day Seattle alternative

Ferry to Bainbridge Island

A scenic, lower-density day built around the water: walk onto the ferry from downtown Seattle, explore Bainbridge on foot, and return the same way. This is a genuine replacement for the MOPOP/anime loop—not an add-on—because the crossing, island time, and return consume most of the day.

~35-minute crossing each way Adult walk-on: $11.35 · youth 18 and under: free Walk-on recommended · check sailing-day alerts Official schedule ↗ Official fares ↗

Flat river backup

Lime Kiln Trail

Mostly gentle river walking, a historic lime kiln, and a natural picnic point. It is longer on paper but comparatively gentle because the route is relatively flat.

Scenic island alternative

Bainbridge Ferry + Bloedel Reserve

A slower, more distinctive visitor day: take the ferry from downtown Seattle, explore Bloedel’s gardens and forest trails, then return via Winslow. This replaces the waterfall hike and gives the group a beautiful Northwest day without a strenuous climb.
